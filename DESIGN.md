## Brand & Style

The design system embodies warm hospitality, artisanal craft, and culinary distinction. Built for a dual-concept fine dining restaurant and heritage bakery, the visual language balances the tactile, oven-warmed charm of artisanal baking with the poised, immaculate elegance of evening gastronomic dining.

The target audience comprises discerning culinary enthusiasts, neighbourhood regulars seeking artisanal sourdough and viennoiserie, and patrons reserving tables for celebratory milestones. The UI evokes warmth, craftsmanship, and understated luxury through generous spatial composition, refined serif display typography, and tactile surface tones reminiscent of unbleached linen, toasted crusts, and polished aged oak.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Editorial Luxury** with **Subtle Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous cream-based negative space that breathes like a crisp linen menu.
- Delicate architectural dividers, filigree ornaments, and subtle borders framing gastronomic photography.
- High-contrast editorial pacing that shifts gracefully from daytime patisserie freshness to moody candlelit evening service.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the sensory world of baking and culinary arts: buttery glazes, caramelized crusts, glowing candlelight, and espresso roast.

- **Primary (`#B37A2B` - Burnished Honey / Warm Amber):** The key action color representing golden crusts and warm dining ambience. Applied to primary calls-to-action, category highlights, and active states.
- **Secondary (`#A24B31` - Terracotta / Baked Clay):** A grounding accent derived from wood-fired ovens and roasted aromatics. Reserved for promotional badges, chef's specials, and subtle hover emphasis.
- **Tertiary (`#D19F53` - Golden Glaze):** A soft, radiant gold used for decorative filigrees, star ratings, and celebratory accents.
- **Neutral (`#1E1A17` - Deep Espresso Roast):** High-density charcoal-brown serving as the primary text foundation instead of stark pure black, maintaining warmth across all text hierarchies.
- **Background & Canvas:** Crisp yet warm off-white linen tones (`#FBF9F5` base canvas, `#F4EFE6` secondary panels, and `#FFFFFF` elevated cards).

## Typography

The typography system pairs **Playfair Display**—a classic, high-contrast transitional serif that channels classic European menu design and culinary heritage—with **Plus Jakarta Sans**, a clean, balanced sans-serif offering maximum legibility for dish descriptions, ingredient lists, allergy tags, and transactional interfaces.

### Hierarchy Rules
- **Eyebrow & Subheadings:** Set in `subheading` using all-caps with generous tracking (`0.12em`) in primary burnished amber or deep espresso to introduce sections (e.g., "EXPERIENCE FINE DINING", "CHEF'S RECOMMENDATIONS").
- **Menu Item Titles:** Rendered in `headline-sm` with Playfair Display to maintain artisanal character even in condensed cards.
- **Pricing:** Set in `price` (Playfair Display, Bold) for a classic restaurant bill-of-fare presentation.
- **Narrative and Descriptions:** Kept in `body-md` with soft charcoal ink (`#4A443E`) ensuring effortless scannability against cream backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point structural system, maintaining an expansive, unhurried cadence appropriate for fine hospitality.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container of 1280px, centered with `margin: 3rem` (expanding on ultra-wide viewports) and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.

### Visual Density & Section Flow
- Editorial banners and hero sections rely on asymmetric split arrangements: left-aligned narrative text countered by framed, high-chroma circular or organically cropped food photography.
- Menu category navigation is organized as a smooth horizontal reel on mobile and a centered circular pill-cluster on desktop.
- Generous vertical padding (`space-2xl` / 4rem) separates curated menu sections, punctuated by subtle botanical or crown filigree center dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy emphasizes organic physical surfaces over deep synthetic shadows.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FBF9F5` warm unbleached linen tone. Flat and tactile.
- **Level 1 (Menu Cards & Panels):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) containers resting on the base canvas. Outlined with a feather-light hairline border (`1px solid rgba(179, 122, 43, 0.12)`) and an ambient, warm amber diffusion: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(78, 52, 24, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Modals):** Lifted state for dish cards and table booking flyouts: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(78, 52, 24, 0.10)`, with border shifting to `rgba(179, 122, 43, 0.25)`.
- **Special Tier (Dark Roast Banners):** Full-bleed immersive strips (such as special offers and reservation anchors) utilize `#1E1A17` (Deep Espresso) with subtle inner glows simulating low-lit dining tables and candle embers.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, soft architectural profile (`roundedness: 1` — 0.25rem base radius). This restraint creates tailored elegance reminiscent of printed menu cardboards, linen labels, and crisp bakery boxes.

- **Standard Buttons & Inputs:** `0.25rem` (4px) rounded corners, ensuring precision and bespoke structure.
- **Menu Item Dish Cards:** `0.5rem` (8px / `rounded-lg`) to gently frame photography and prevent sharp digital clipping.
- **Category Avatars:** Perfect circular shapes (`50%` / full round) for dish categories (Starters, Bakery, Mains, Desserts) echoing ceramic tableware plates.
- **Badges ("Chef's Special", "Freshly Baked"):** `0.25rem` (4px) with subtle internal padding for a stamp-like look.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Book a Table", "Order Now"):** Solid `#B37A2B` (Burnished Honey) background with pure white typography (`label-md`), uppercase tracking, 4px border radius, and `12px 28px` padding. Transitions on hover to `#9B6520` with a subtle amber shadow.
- **Secondary / Outline Action (e.g., "Explore Menu"):** Transparent background with a `1.5px solid #1E1A17` border and `#1E1A17` text. Transitions to solid `#1E1A17` fill with white text on hover.
- **Tertiary Accent:** Soft terracotta fill (`#A24B31`) for seasonal specials and limited bakery releases.

### Cards & Menu Listings
- **Dish Cards:** Crisp white surface with an integrated high-aspect photography top section (`rounded-t` to match outer card). Padding of `space-md` housing dish title in `headline-sm`, subtitle/key ingredients in `body-sm` muted espresso (`#635C55`), star rating cluster in `#D19F53`, and price pinned cleanly to the bottom right in `price` serif format.
- **Category Disc Cards:** Centered circular imagery styled with a soft warm border ring (`2px solid #EADDC9`), title centered beneath in `label-md`.

### Navigation & Chips
- **Category Filter Chips (e.g., "All Items", "Artisan Breads", "Starters"):** Unselected tabs feature clear underline-only indicators or naked text in `#635C55`. The active tab adopts an espresso or burnished amber under-dash or filled soft-amber capsule with bold text.
- **Top Utility Header:** Slim off-white or deep-espresso bar featuring telephone, email, and social icons, framed by a delicate divider line.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Reservation Fields (Date, Guests, Time):** `#FFFFFF` background with a subtle border (`1px solid #D8CEBE`). Focus state brings a warm amber glow ring (`1.5px solid #B37A2B`). Typography utilizes `body-md` in deep espresso.

### Badges & Labels
- **Artisan / Special Badges:** Compact tags positioned absolute top-left on photography cards. "Bestseller" uses burnished gold (`#B37A2B`), while "New" or "Chef's Special" uses baked terracotta (`#A24B31`). Text is white, bold, uppercase (`label-sm`).
